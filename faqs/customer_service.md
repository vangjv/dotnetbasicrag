# SPAM Customer Service FAQ

## How can I contact Hormel about SPAM?
- Phone (U.S.): 1-800-523-4635 (Mon-Fri 8 AM–4 PM CT)  
- Email: consumer@hormel.com  
- Mailing: Hormel Foods Consumer Response, 1 Hormel Pl, Austin, MN 55912

## My can arrived dented—can I still eat it?
If a dent is shallow and the seam isn’t compromised, the product is safe. Deep dents on seams may allow bacterial entry—dispose of the can and contact customer service for replacement.

## The pull tab broke. How do I open the can?
Use a standard can opener on the bottom rim. If unavailable, contact customer service for a coupon.

## What if I have an allergic reaction?
Discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional. Then report details to Hormel’s safety hotline: 1-800-523-4635.

## Do you ship SPAM directly to consumers?
Hormel does not sell direct, but partners like Walmart, Amazon, and SpamMuseum.com offer online ordering.

## How do I request donations or sponsorship?
Submit a proposal via Hormel’s Community Giving portal at hormelfoods.com/about/community/.
