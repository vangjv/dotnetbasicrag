# SPAM Recipes & Pairings FAQ

## Classic quick breakfast idea?
Slice SPAM into ¼-inch pieces, pan-fry until crisp, and serve with scrambled eggs and rice (Hawaiian-style).

## Can I use SPAM in sushi?
Yes—SPAM musubi is popular in Hawaii. Press sushi rice in a musubi mold, top with a teriyaki-glazed SPAM slice, and wrap with nori.

## Vegetarian ersatz?
No direct vegetarian SPAM exists, but diced mushrooms sautéed with soy sauce mimic umami and texture in recipes calling for SPAM.

## Recommended wine pairing?
Off-dry Riesling balances SPAM’s saltiness; for beer, a crisp pilsner works well.

## Best way to cube SPAM evenly?
Chill the can, eject the loaf, and use a ½-inch-grid vegetable chopper or slice both directions with a sharp knife.

## Dessert featuring SPAM—seriously?
Caramelized SPAM bits sprinkled over vanilla ice cream is a novelty served at the SPAM Museum café (sweet-salty contrast).
