# SPAM Product FAQ

## What exactly is SPAM?
SPAM is a shelf-stable cooked pork product made primarily from pork shoulder and ham, combined with salt, water, potato starch, sugar, and sodium nitrite for color and preservation.

## Is it fully cooked?
Yes. Every can of SPAM is fully cooked at the Hormel Foods plant. You can eat it straight from the can or heat it in any preferred way.

## How long does an unopened can last?
The “Best By” date stamped on the bottom is typically 3 years from the production date. Store in a cool, dry place for maximum shelf life.

## How do I read the code on top of the can?
The first two numbers represent the month, the next two numbers represent the day, and the last two numbers represent the year of production.

## Are there different varieties?
Yes—Classic, Lite, 25% Less Sodium, Hickory Smoke, Jalapeño, Teriyaki, Hot & Spicy, Turkey, and several limited-time regional flavors.

## Is the can recyclable?
Yes. Both the metal can and the paper label are recyclable in most municipal programs.
