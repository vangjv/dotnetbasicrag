# SPAM Brand History FAQ

## When was SPAM first introduced?
SPAM was launched by Hormel Foods on July 5, 1937.

## Why was it created?
Hormel wanted an affordable, shelf-stable meat that required no refrigeration during the Great Depression.

## How did SPAM become popular worldwide?
Large quantities were shipped to Allied troops during World War II. Post-war, soldiers introduced it to Europe and the Pacific, where it remains popular.

## Where does the name “SPAM” come from?
Officially it’s a portmanteau of “Spiced Ham.” A Hormel employee won $100 in a 1937 naming contest.

## Has the recipe changed?
Only slightly—potato starch was added in 2009 to reduce gelatin separation. Core ingredients remain the same.

## Does Hormel still make SPAM in Austin, Minnesota?
Yes. The original Austin plant is the primary U.S. production site, complemented by facilities in Nebraska and Iowa.
