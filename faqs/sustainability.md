# SPAM Sustainability & Sourcing FAQ

## Where does the pork come from?
Hormel sources pigs from independent family farms in the Midwest that follow the company’s Animal Care and Quality Assurance Program.

## What is Hormel doing to reduce greenhouse-gas emissions?
Hormel has committed to science-based targets—a 30 % absolute reduction in Scope 1 & 2 GHG emissions by 2030 (from a 2019 baseline).

## Is the metal can made from recycled content?
Yes. On average, SPAM cans contain 28 % post-consumer recycled steel and are 100 % recyclable.

## How efficient is water usage in production?
Hormel reports ~2.5 gallons of water per pound of finished product, down 15 % since 2012.

## Are any SPAM varieties Certified Humane or organic?
Not currently. Hormel is exploring third-party welfare certifications for select premium lines but none have launched yet.

## What about palm oil?
SPAM contains no palm-oil ingredients.
