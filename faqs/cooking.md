# SPAM Cooking & Storage FAQ

## Best way to slice SPAM?
Chill the can, open both ends, push the loaf through, then use a sharp chef’s knife or wire egg slicer for uniform slices.

## How do I crisp it?
Pan-fry over medium-high heat for ~2 minutes per side. For extra-crisp edges, score the surface with shallow cross-hatch cuts before frying.

## Can I air-fry SPAM?
Yes. 400 °F / 200 °C for 6–8 minutes, flipping halfway, yields crispy cubes great for salads or fried rice.

## Safe internal temperature?
Because SPAM is fully cooked, heating to 165 °F (74 °C) is sufficient but not required for safety—heat to preferred taste.

## How long will an opened can last in the fridge?
Transfer leftovers to a sealed container and use within 3–4 days.

## Can I freeze SPAM?
You can, but freezing may affect texture. Wrap tightly, freeze up to 3 months, and thaw in the refrigerator.
