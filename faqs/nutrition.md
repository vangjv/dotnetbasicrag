# SPAM Nutrition FAQ

## What are the nutrition facts for one serving?
A 2 oz (56 g) Classic SPAM serving contains:
- 180 calories  
- 16 g fat (6 g saturated)  
- 790 mg sodium  
- 1 g total carb (0 g sugar)  
- 7 g protein  

## Does SPAM contain gluten?
All US-distributed SPAM varieties are gluten-free.

## Is SPAM keto-friendly?
Classic SPAM has only 1 g carbohydrate per serving, fitting most low-carb and keto guidelines.

## Does SPAM contain any common allergens?
SPAM contains no dairy, eggs, fish, shellfish, nuts, or soy. It does contain pork and is produced in facilities that handle other meats.

## Is there a low-sodium option?
Yes—SPAM 25% Less Sodium contains about 580 mg sodium per 2 oz serving.

## What about nitrites?
SPAM uses sodium nitrite, an FDA-approved curing agent that prevents bacterial growth and helps preserve color.
